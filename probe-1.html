Conviction Over Reward

You are reluctant to devote more time because, somewhere, you don't believe that any job deserves that much of your effort. But when it is a matter of pride, as in sports, you find devoting that time plausible because it is something you genuinely enjoy.

But I am simply telling you what I personally believe.

It doesn't matter to me whether I am preparing for a bank clerical examination or a cricket match. I will prepare with the same intensity, whatever the rewards may be.

Because, for me, nothing is little—not the wonder of Sachin scoring a magnificent century for India, applauded by millions, nor the dignified work of a clerk who performs his duties with utmost professionalism, silently contributing to nation-building, however small his individual contribution may seem.

For me, conviction in a job far exceeds the fruits it may bear.
